# Example Categories — San Antonio Print Shop

This is example/reference configuration for a downtown San Antonio print-shop lead pack. Copy ideas from it; do not treat it as universal default config.

**Primary market**: San Antonio, TX — ZIP 78205 (downtown core: The Alamo, River Walk).

## Parking

**Typical business types**: parking garages, surface parking lots, valet services, parking management companies, event parking operators

**Pitch notes**: Rate signage, directional/wayfinding signs, entrance and exit banners, “Lot Full” and validation signage, durable outdoor-rated placards. Downtown tourist traffic means signage needs to be clear to out-of-towners and weatherproof year-round.

## Tourism & Attractions

**Typical business types**: tour operators, museums, historic sites, visitor centers, ticket booths, attraction gift shops

**Pitch notes**: Rack cards, trifold brochures, sandwich-board sidewalk signage, ticket-booth window graphics, banners for seasonal exhibits or special events. Emphasize quick-turn reprints for schedule changes and multi-language signage options.

## Restaurants

**Typical business types**: restaurants, cafes, bars, food trucks, quick-service restaurants, fine dining

**Pitch notes**: Laminated menus, table tents, window decals, sandwich boards, patio banners, illuminated signage. Emphasize durability and fast reprint turnaround for seasonal or limited-time menu changes.

## Shopping & Retail

**Typical business types**: souvenir shops, boutiques, downtown retail, shopping centers, malls, specialty stores

**Pitch notes**: Storefront window graphics, sale/promo banners, sidewalk A-frame signs, seasonal display signage, grand-opening banners. Repeat-order pricing fits stores running frequent seasonal promotions.

## Advertising & Marketing Agencies

**Typical business types**: local ad agencies, marketing firms, event promoters, tourism boards, chambers of commerce, media buyers

**Pitch notes**: Position as a fast local print-production partner for agencies that need reliable fulfillment on client campaigns: banners, large-format posters, trade-show displays, and direct mail.
